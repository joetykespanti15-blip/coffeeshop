Bean & Brew

Our coffee shop welcomes you with a warm atmosphere and a commitment to ethically sourced, small-batch roasted beans that truly stand out from the competition. We are best known for our signature Cold Brew and velvety Flat Whites, which have become neighborhood favorites since we first opened our doors. The business began as a small garage passion project in 2010 and has since grown into a beloved community hub built on a foundation of quality and ritual. Every cup we serve tells the story of our journey from humble beginnings to a premier destination for coffee enthusiasts..
